Introduction
  • Expense Management System is a full-stack MERN web application engineered for comprehensive personal financial tracking.
  • It logs and monitors daily income, salary, recurring bills, and granular discretionary expenses.
  • It empowers individuals and households to track cash-flow patterns, prevent impulsive overspending, and maintain long-term financial discipline.
  • The platform delivers real-time categorical summaries, interactive visualization charts (Bar, Line, Pie), and multi-criteria transaction sorting.
Problem Statement

In personal finance management, traditional manual bookkeeping through paper ledgers, mobile memos, or raw spreadsheets is tedious, error-prone, and unsustainable. Individuals lack consolidated visibility into their historical spending velocity across disparate expenditure categories.

The proposed Expense Management System leverages modern MERN architecture, cloud document storage (MongoDB Atlas), and automated chart analytics to eliminate manual bookkeeping friction, providing real-time financial transparency and data-driven budgeting.

Objectives
1
To engineer a secure, responsive MERN-stack web portal for daily expense logging and income management.
2
To implement complete CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) for multi-category financial records.
3
To provide interactive visual analytics (Bar, Line, Pie, and Circular Progress gauges) powered by Chart.js.
4
To enable granular multi-attribute filtering (by category) and bidirectional sorting (by date and amount).
5
To deploy a decoupled production architecture on Vercel (Frontend) and Render (Backend) with 24/7 automated keep-alive.
